The Risks of Public Job Applications

Published on January 07, 2026 • 2 min read

Posting your Gmail address while also complaining about fake job alerts on LinkedIn can be contradictory—sometimes, we unintentionally encourage the problem. Instead, why not apply directly or send a DM where possible?

The Trap of "Interested" Comments

Some job ads ask people to comment “Interested,” even when the roles may not be genuine. Regardless of their authenticity, such posts still achieve the traffic the poster is seeking. Others request that applicants drop their email addresses in the comments. While this may be well-intentioned, it also exposes you to potential risks.

Security Risks

Malicious documents can be sent to your email, potentially exposing your device to viruses or trojans. Stay alert, avoid unnecessary exposure, and if you must share your email, carefully scrutinize every sender, email address, and link before clicking.
